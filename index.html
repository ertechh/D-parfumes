The House

Composed in London,
in small batches.

Dee Parfums is an independent perfumery devoted to rare ingredients and slow craft. Each fragrance is composed in our atelier, bottled by hand, and finished with a hand-numbered label.

We work with growers in Grasse, Tuscany and the Comoros to source the finest naturals — then let them speak quietly. Nothing overstated. Nothing rushed.
